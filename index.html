What powers it?

It runs on 5V USB. Plug it into a phone charger, laptop, power bank or car charger. No batteries needed.

Does it need a dark room?

It looks best with the lights off or dimmed. In a fully lit room the projection is much softer.

Orders ship from Myrtle Beach, SC within 1–2 business days, and most arrive 2–5 business days after that. Order by December 15 to get it before Christmas.
